Call for Doctoral Symposium and Early Career Track

The 27th IEEE International Symposium on Cluster, Cloud, and Internet Computing (CCGrid 2027) invites participants to submit original and unpublished work to the CCGrid 2027 Doctoral Symposium and Early Career Track.

Contributions may include preliminary approaches and results, novel work in progress, or a review of the recent state of the art. Submissions are welcome in all areas aligned with the CCGrid 2027 technical topics described in the Call for Conference Research Papers.

Eligibility

Eligibility for the CCGrid 2027 Doctoral Symposium and Early Career Track requires first authors to satisfy one of the following criteria:

  • Doctoral students enrolled in a doctoral degree program at an academic institution.
  • Early-career researchers within four years of their Ph.D. graduation, with an official degree awarded in December 2022 or later.

Exceptional circumstances, such as parental leave or caregiving responsibilities, will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Submission Guidelines

  • Submitted manuscripts should be structured as technical papers and may not exceed four letter-size (8.5 × 11 inch) pages, including references, figures, and tables, using the IEEE conference template.
  • Author and reviewer anonymity: All submissions must be single-blind. Manuscripts should include sufficient detail to facilitate thorough review and serve as a reference for future research.
  • Track selection: Authors should select the “IEEE CCGrid 2027 Doctoral Symposium and Early Career Track” to ensure correct assignment, as multiple technical tracks are open concurrently.
  • Review criteria: Manuscripts will be judged on novelty, technical strength, correctness, presentation quality, and relevance to conference attendees.
  • Accepted papers will be submitted for possible inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements.

Important Dates

  • Paper submission:February 15, 2027
  • Notification of Acceptance:March 8, 2027
  • Camera ready:March 16, 2027

Presentation

  • Authors of accepted papers will be allocated short speaking slots to present their work as part of a session at the main conference.
  • Participants are also expected to present their work at an interactive poster session and talk one-on-one with attendees. The recommended poster size is a maximum of A0 portrait (841 × 1189 mm) or A1 portrait (594 × 841 mm).
  • At least one student or early-career author of each accepted paper must register for the conference at the author registration rate and present the work. Any no-shows will cause the accepted contribution to be withdrawn from the conference proceedings.
