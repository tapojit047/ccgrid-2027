CCGrid 2027 Patron Program

Partner with CCGrid 2027

The IEEE International Symposium on Cluster, Cloud, and Internet Computing (CCGrid) is a leading international forum for distributed systems, cloud computing, edge computing, and emerging computing technologies. Becoming a patron of CCGrid 2027 offers an opportunity to strengthen your organization’s visibility, connect with global research and industry leaders, and engage outstanding technical talent.

CCGrid 2027 will take place May 24–27, 2027, in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas, USA. Patron packages are available for organizations of different sizes and priorities, from conference recognition to premium visibility, recruiting opportunities, and named support of conference activities.

Patron Levels & Benefits

Diamond

$20,000+
  • Highest-level recognition across the conference website, program, venue signage, and conference communications
  • Up to four complimentary conference registrations
  • A recruiting or exhibit table
  • Named recognition as the Conference Dinner Sponsor
  • Option to direct support toward student participation or another named conference initiative
  • A commemorative plaque and patron certificate

Platinum

$10,000–$19,999
  • Premium visibility across the conference website, program, venue signage, and conference communications
  • Up to four complimentary conference registrations
  • A recruiting or exhibit table
  • Named support of an eligible lunch activity, student program, or other conference event
  • A commemorative plaque and patron certificate

Gold

$5,000–$9,999
  • Recognition on the conference website and patron materials
  • Two complimentary conference registrations
  • A recruiting or exhibit table
  • A patron certificate

Silver

$2,500–$4,999
  • Recognition on the conference website and patron materials
  • One complimentary conference registration
  • A patron certificate

Bronze

$1,000–$2,499
  • Recognition on the conference website and patron materials
  • A patron certificate

Recognition for Every Patron

  • Every supporting organization will receive a patron certificate.
  • Platinum and Diamond patrons will also receive a commemorative plaque.
  • Diamond-level support will receive Conference Dinner Sponsor recognition.
  • Eligible non-Diamond named support will generally be associated with lunch activities, student support, or another available conference program element.

Alternative Support Opportunities

Organizations may also discuss support for specific activities or in-kind contributions. Opportunities may include student registrations or travel support, coffee breaks, lunch activities, the conference reception, the conference dinner, recruiting activities, or other mutually agreed conference needs.

Why Support CCGrid?

  • Showcase your organization to an international audience of researchers, practitioners, students, and industry leaders.
  • Demonstrate thought leadership in cloud, edge, distributed systems, AI infrastructure, and emerging computing technologies.
  • Engage graduate students and early-career researchers who represent the next generation of technical talent.
  • Support student participation and global research exchange at a long-running IEEE conference.

Become a CCGrid 2027 Patron

Sponsorship commitments for CCGrid 2027 will be accepted through mid-April 2027.

To discuss a patron package, named activity, or in-kind contribution, please email the CCGrid 2027 organizing team at ccgrid2027@gmail.com.
